Profile

wave to earth is a South Korean trio making dreamy, jazz-tinged indie. Most of their songs are in English, some in Korean. Their signature song “seasons” has passed 500 million streams on Spotify.

Daniel Kim

Vocals, guitar

Dongkyu Shin

Drums

John Cha

Bass

History

  • 2019
    The trio debuts with the single “wave” in August.
  • 2020
    The EP “wave 0.01” arrives in January, followed by “summer flows 0.02” in August.
  • 2021
    The band signs with the label WAVY.
  • 2023
    First full-length album, “flaws and all” (April), with “bad”, “calla” and “dried flower”, followed by a sold-out North American tour.
  • 2024
    The limited LP “uncounted 0.00” in May and the EP “play with earth! 0.03” in September, then another sold-out North American tour.
  • 2026
    Three sold-out nights at Olympic Hall in Seoul in February, then the second album “bad pieces” in August and the pieces tour.
